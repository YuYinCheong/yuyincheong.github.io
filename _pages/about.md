I am a Statistics undergraduate at the University of North Florida, with a concentration in Actuarial Science and minors in Computing and Leadership. I expect to graduate in December 2026. My academic and professional interests include data science, data engineering, and artificial intelligence.

My work combines statistical analysis with practical computing, from organizing and standardizing data to developing models and interpreting results.

## Research

I serve as a research assistant in the Chalk-Carey Research Group at UNF, working with biomedical datasets related to the microbiome and metabolomics. My responsibilities include processing and standardizing data, designing relational database structures, and developing workflows using Python, Django, pandas, and MySQL.

This work focuses on improving data quality and organization to support downstream analysis and research.

## Selected Projects

**Housing Price Analysis**  
A multiple linear regression analysis examining how property characteristics are associated with housing prices. The project includes exploratory data analysis, variable transformations, model diagnostics, and robust statistical inference.

**Mayo Clinic Datathon**  
A collaborative project analyzing clinical data from the MIMIC-IV dataset to investigate healthcare questions through data analysis and machine learning.

## Leadership

I am the founder and president of the Actuarial Students Association at UNF, supporting peer learning, student engagement, and professional development.
